What's new

• Rev for Linux: the same Rev, now for Linux Mint (and Ubuntu, Debian and most other 64-bit Linux). The same screens, keys, colours, decks, review, stats, Anki import and export, backups and sync. Light and dark mode follow your desktop.
• On Linux Mint: download rev-flashcards_1.3.0_amd64.deb and double-click it to install; Rev appears in the menu under Education. Other Linux: Rev-1.3.0-linux-x64.tar.gz, unpack it and run ./Rev. The step-by-step guide (moving your decks from Windows, Anki and the Rev Sync add-on on Linux, pairing your iPhone over Wi-Fi) is docs/LINUX.md in the repository.
• Moving from Windows: in Rev for Windows, Settings → Backup → Export backup; in Rev for Linux, Settings → Backup → Restore from backup. Everything comes across, including your progress and history.
• Sync on Linux: Linux has no iCloud Drive, so the Rev Sync add-on uses a "Rev Sync" folder in your home folder, and Rev on iPhone syncs with Anki over your home Wi-Fi.
• Rev for Windows: no visible changes. It finds the Rev Sync add-on's folder setting on more setups.

Rev for Windows isn't signed with a paid certificate, so the first time you open the installer Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click More info → Run anyway. Rev installs just for you (no administrator needed), and uninstalling it from Settings → Apps keeps your decks.
