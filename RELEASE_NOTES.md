What's new

• Custom study: click "Custom study" inside a deck, or on the "All caught up!" screen, to study more than today's queue. Choose one of four options:
  – More new cards: learn new cards beyond today's limit.
  – Forgotten cards: go over every card you pressed Again on in the last few days.
  – Review ahead: review cards that come due in the next few days, for example before an exam.
  – Study by tag: pick one or more tags and choose all, new or studied cards, how many, and random or oldest-first order.
• Answers in custom study count as normal reviews and reschedule the cards, and they sync with Anki and Rev on iPhone as usual.
• The same on Windows and Linux.

Rev for Windows isn't signed with a paid certificate, so the first time you open the installer Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click More info → Run anyway. Rev installs just for you (no administrator needed), and uninstalling it from Settings → Apps keeps your decks.
