What's new

• First version of Rev for Windows: the desktop sibling of Rev for iPhone.
• Home screen: a greeting, today's Due / New / Done numbers, and your decks as big colourful tiles with "cards · due · new" counts.
• Create, rename and delete decks (Ctrl+N, F2 and Delete work too). Deleting always asks first and shows how many cards will go.
• Inside a deck: its numbers, Add card, Paste many, and the list of its cards (click one to edit it).
• Four kinds of card: Basic, Basic + reversed (2 cards), Cloze with a “word to hide” helper (one card per number), and Type the answer.
• Fast card entry: Save & next (Ctrl+Enter) keeps your card kind and tags, tag suggestions are one click away, and Paste many turns “front | back” lines into cards.
• Bold and italic: select words and press Ctrl+B or Ctrl+I (or click B / I). Cards show real formatting.
• Studying: a calm review screen with new / learning / review counts, and four big buttons (Again · Hard · Good · Easy) that show when you'll see the card next. Study one deck, or Review all decks.
• Keyboard first: Space shows the answer, 1–4 rate, Ctrl+Z undoes, E edits the card, Esc goes back. Clicking works everywhere too.
• Undo puts the card back exactly as it was. Edit a card mid-review without losing your place.
• Leeches: forget a card for the 8th time and Rev offers to help you rewrite or suspend it.
• Type the answer cards check what you typed (capitals, extra spaces and a final full stop don't matter).
• Lists keep their bullets and numbers, and images can be clicked to zoom (scroll to zoom in, drag to move).
• The same scheduling as Rev on iPhone: FSRS-5 with an adjustable memory target, or classic SM-2, with learning steps, overdue credit, interval fuzz, sibling burying, a 4 am day rollover and leech detection.
• Rev's warm colours, rounded Nunito text and comic icon, in light and dark mode (follows your Windows setting).
