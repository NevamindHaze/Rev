What's new

• Faster sync at home: Rev for Windows now notices within seconds when Anki changes something, and syncs by itself. If you're in the middle of a review, it syncs right after.
• Together with the new sync over Wi-Fi between Rev on iPhone and Anki, a change on your iPhone reaches Rev for Windows in a few seconds when you're at home with Anki open, instead of waiting for iCloud.

Note: this works best with the newest Rev Sync add-on in Anki (copy the new anki-addon/rev_sync folder from the Rev repository into Anki's addons21 folder and restart Anki) and the newest Rev on iPhone. For Wi-Fi, in Anki open Tools → Rev Sync Wi-Fi…, and on the iPhone Settings → Sync over Wi-Fi.

Rev isn't signed with a paid certificate, so the first time you open the installer Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click More info → Run anyway. Rev installs just for you (no administrator needed), and uninstalling it from Settings → Apps keeps your decks.
