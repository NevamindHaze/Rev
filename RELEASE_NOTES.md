What's new

• Cards from Anki whose bold or italic runs over several lines (or a whole list) now show it properly, instead of stray ** on lines of their own. Cards already on your phone are fixed at the next sync with Anki, unless you've edited them in Rev.
• Signal: each deck on the Decks screen now has a coloured dot in front of its name.
• New look to try: Signal, a dark theme with big numbers and lime accents. Turn it on in Settings → Appearance. The app stays as it is until you do, and you can switch back any time.
