What's new

• First version of Rev for Windows: the desktop sibling of Rev for iPhone.
• Home screen: a greeting, today's Due / New / Done numbers, and your decks as big colourful tiles with "cards · due · new" counts.
• Create, rename and delete decks (Ctrl+N, F2 and Delete work too). Deleting always asks first and shows how many cards will go.
• Inside a deck: its numbers, Add card, Paste many, and the list of its cards (click one to edit it).
• Four kinds of card: Basic, Basic + reversed (2 cards), Cloze with a “word to hide” helper (one card per number), and Type the answer.
• Fast card entry: Save & next (Ctrl+Enter) keeps your card kind and tags, tag suggestions are one click away, and Paste many turns “front | back” lines into cards.
• Bold and italic: select words and press Ctrl+B or Ctrl+I (or click B / I). Cards show real formatting.
• Card browser, inside each deck and for All cards (Ctrl+F): search questions, answers and tags; show only due, new, learning, review, leech or suspended cards, or one tag; sort by newest, oldest, due soonest, A to Z, most forgotten or hardest.
• Each card shows badges for its state, kind, leech and suspended. Right-click it (or click ⋯) to edit, add or remove tags, suspend or unsuspend, clear the leech mark, or delete it (always asks first).
• Studying: a calm review screen with new / learning / review counts, and four big buttons (Again · Hard · Good · Easy) that show when you'll see the card next. Study one deck, or Review all decks.
• Keyboard first: Space shows the answer, 1–4 rate, Ctrl+Z undoes, E edits the card, Esc goes back. Clicking works everywhere too.
• Undo puts the card back exactly as it was. Edit a card mid-review without losing your place.
• Leeches: forget a card for the 8th time and Rev offers to help you rewrite or suspend it.
• Type the answer cards check what you typed (capitals, extra spaces and a final full stop don't matter).
• Lists keep their bullets and numbers, and images can be clicked to zoom (scroll to zoom in, drag to move).
• Stats: cards due today, your streak, reviewed today, new and total cards, and a chart of the last 7 days (today in coral).
• Settings: your name for the greeting, new cards per day, FSRS or SM-2, and FSRS's target memory (80–97%). Changes are saved straight away.
• Anki import (Settings → Anki): any .apkg or .colpkg, old or newest format. Text keeps its bold, italic and lists; images, tags, study progress (with FSRS memory), suspended cards and leeches come across, and sub-decks keep their names. Importing the same deck again updates your cards instead of adding copies. Your Anki files are only read, never changed.
• Anki export: save one deck (inside the deck) or all decks (Settings → Anki) as a .apkg that any Anki can open. Schedules (with FSRS memory), tags, images, leeches and suspended cards come along, and exporting again updates the same notes in Anki instead of adding copies.
• Backup and restore (Settings → Backup): save everything to a .json file, or restore one. Restoring asks first, then replaces everything; your current data is saved in the Backups folder first, just in case. Backups from Rev on iPhone work too.
• The same scheduling as Rev on iPhone: FSRS-5 with an adjustable memory target, or classic SM-2, with learning steps, overdue credit, interval fuzz, sibling burying, a 4 am day rollover and leech detection.
• Rev's warm colours, rounded Nunito text and comic icon, in light and dark mode (follows your Windows setting).

Note: Rev isn't signed with a paid certificate, so the first time you open the installer Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click More info → Run anyway. Rev installs just for you (no administrator needed), and uninstalling it from Settings → Apps keeps your decks.
