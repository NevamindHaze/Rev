What's new

• Sync with Anki: Rev and Anki on your computer now stay in step automatically, both ways — reviews, new cards, edits, tags, suspended cards, deletions and images. Set it up once in Settings › Sync with Anki by choosing the “Rev Sync” folder in iCloud Drive.
• Rev syncs by itself whenever you open it or leave it, and there's a “Sync now” button with the time of the last sync.
• For each card, whichever app you used most recently wins, and review history from both apps is kept, so your stats and streak include everything.

Improvements
• Imported Anki cards now remember exactly which Anki card they came from, so re-importing and syncing never create duplicates.
