What's new

• New look: a comic-style app icon.
• Card types: Basic, Basic + reversed, Cloze (fill in the blank) and Type the answer.
• Tags: add tags while creating cards; tap a suggested tag to reuse it.
• Card browser: filter cards by state or tag and sort them (due soonest, most forgotten, hardest…). Use “All cards” (🔍 on the Decks screen) to search every deck at once.
• Images: Anki images now import, show on cards, and open full screen with pinch-to-zoom.
• Anki import works with any .apkg or .colpkg, including Anki's newest format.
• Leech detection: cards you keep forgetting are flagged, with options to rewrite or suspend them.

Improvements
• Long list items now wrap neatly under their text.
• Re-importing a deck updates your existing cards (and their tags) instead of adding copies.
