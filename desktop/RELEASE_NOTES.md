What's new

• Rev for Linux is laid out for a desktop: your decks are always in a sidebar, cards are in a sortable table, studying happens in the window, and buttons and text are desktop-sized. Ctrl+1/2/3 open Home, All cards and Stats; Ctrl+B hides the sidebar.
• On Linux, bold and italic show while you edit a card: select words, press Ctrl+B or Ctrl+I, and they turn bold or italic right away, with the stars hidden. The arrow keys, Backspace and Delete step over the hidden stars, and Ctrl+Z undoes.
• Pressing Italic on a bold word (or Bold on an italic one) no longer swaps one for the other.

Rev for Windows isn't signed with a paid certificate, so the first time you open the installer Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click More info → Run anyway. Rev installs just for you (no administrator needed), and uninstalling it from Settings → Apps keeps your decks.
