What's new

• Cards from Anki whose bold or italic runs over several lines (or a whole list) now show it properly, instead of stray ** on lines of their own. This applies to cards imported or synced from now on; cards already in Rev keep the text they have.
• On Linux, Rev can now update along with the rest of your system: after a one-time setup (see desktop/docs/LINUX.md), sudo apt update && sudo apt upgrade brings each new version, and your decks stay.

Rev for Windows isn't signed with a paid certificate, so the first time you open the installer Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". Click More info → Run anyway. Rev installs just for you (no administrator needed), and uninstalling it from Settings → Apps keeps your decks.
